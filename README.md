# SalarySouq — UAE Salary Quick Check

Check UAE salaries and estimate take-home pay in seconds.

SalarySouq helps you know your worth in the UAE. This extension gives you a quick monthly-salary and savings check right from your browser, plus one-click access to SalarySouq's salary guides and calculators. Figures you enter stay on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

## Download

Installable packages are on the [Releases](https://github.com/maria876david-debug/salarysouq-extension/releases) page — Microsoft Edge (.zip) and Firefox (.zip).

## Website

https://salarysouq.com

## Privacy

No data is collected. The extension runs locally in your browser; no account, no tracking, no remote requests.
