# UAE Visa Overstay Fines: How the AED 50-a-Day Fine Adds Up

Overstaying a UAE visa is one of the most expensive small mistakes an expat can make, because the fine is simple, daily and unforgiving: AED 50 for every day you remain in the country after your visa or grace period ends. The rule comes from Cabinet Resolution 89 of 2022, and it applies at a flat rate — there is no discount for a short overstay and no warning period once your time is up.

## How fast the number grows

At AED 50 a day, a week of overstay is AED 350. A month is roughly AED 1,500. Three months runs to about AED 4,500 — before any other costs of changing status or leaving. People are often surprised because the daily figure sounds small; it is the accumulation that hurts, and the fine must be settled before you can regularise your status or depart cleanly.

## Grace periods: who gets extra time

Not everyone starts paying on day one. After a residence visa is cancelled or expires, some categories receive a grace period to leave or change status: up to 180 days for Golden, Green and Blue visa holders, their family members, widows and divorced women, students who have finished their studies, and foreign relatives of citizens; 90 days for skilled professionals in the first three levels and property owners; 60 days for some entry permits issued with a guarantor or host; and 30 days for most other categories. Visit and tourist visas carry no grace period at all — the fine starts the day after the visa ends.

## The paper that matters

Your cancellation paper or visa status document states the date your grace period runs to, and that document — not a friend's experience or an old forum post — is what decides your position. Rules and categories have changed in recent years, so always follow the date on your own paperwork. If you are unsure of your status, the ICP call centre on 600522222 can confirm it.

## Work out your own figure before it grows

If you think you may already be overstaying, do not guess and do not wait. Count the days from the end of your visa or grace period, multiply by AED 50, and treat that as the minimum you need to settle. The fine only ever moves in one direction, and every day of delay adds to it.
