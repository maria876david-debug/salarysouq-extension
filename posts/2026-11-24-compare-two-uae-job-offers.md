# Two UAE Job Offers, Two Salaries: How to Compare Them Properly

Two offers, two headline numbers — and the bigger number is not always the better job. A UAE salary is a package, not a single figure, and comparing offers properly means looking at every line of the package, not just the one printed largest.

## Start with the breakdown, not the total

A UAE offer usually splits pay into basic salary plus allowances — housing, transport, sometimes food or a phone allowance. The split matters more than the total. Your end-of-service gratuity is calculated on your basic salary, not on the total package, so two offers with the same total can produce very different gratuity payouts. Ask for the breakdown in writing before you compare anything else.

## The lines people forget to compare

**Housing.** A housing allowance sounds generous until you check rents in the area you'll actually live in. Some employers provide company accommodation instead — worth less on paper, but it can mean no deposit, no agent fee and no annual rent negotiation.

**Annual flights and leave.** Most UAE contracts include an annual flight home and 30 days of paid annual leave. Check whether flights cover your family too, and whether leave can be carried over or paid out.

**Medical insurance.** Coverage is mandatory in most emirates, but the quality varies enormously. Ask which hospitals are on the network and whether dependants are covered, before you need to find out in an emergency.

**Visa and work permit costs.** The employer pays for your work visa and labour card. If an offer asks you to pay these costs yourself, that is a warning sign, not a normal term.

## The cost-of-living check

The same salary buys a very different life in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah. Rents, school fees and transport costs change the maths completely, so compare each offer against the cost of living where that job actually sits — not against the salary you earned back home.

## Ask these questions before you sign

What is the basic salary, separately from allowances? What does the housing allowance actually cover? Is the annual flight included, and for whom? What is the notice period, and what happens during probation? If the employer can't answer these in writing, the offer isn't finished yet — and neither is your decision.
