# Office Boy Salary in the UAE: What the Job Pays and What Changes It

An office boy is one of the most common entry-level jobs in the UAE, and the pay is not one fixed number. It changes with the city, the size of the employer, and whether accommodation and transport are included in the package or paid separately. A role in a large company in Abu Dhabi can look very different from the same title in a small office elsewhere.

Before accepting any offer, check what the full package is worth - basic salary plus housing, transport and any allowances - and compare it against the current range for the role.
