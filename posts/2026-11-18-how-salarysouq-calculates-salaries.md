# How SalarySouq Calculates Salaries: What Goes Into Every Number

Every salary figure on SalarySouq comes from the same question: what does this job actually pay in the UAE, right now? The calculation starts with reported pay data for the role, then adjusts for the things that move a number in real life - the emirate, the size of the employer, the experience level, and whether housing and transport are included in the package or paid on top. A figure without that context is just a guess, so each guide explains what the range covers and what changes it. SalarySouq now covers 119 professions across the UAE, with 78 guides live and more added every week. The method is written out in full on the site's How We Calculate Salaries page, so anyone can check the working instead of taking a number on trust.
