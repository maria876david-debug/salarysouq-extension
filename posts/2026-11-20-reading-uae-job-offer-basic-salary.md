# Reading a UAE Job Offer: Basic Salary, Allowances and What the Total Really Means

A UAE job offer usually shows one big total - and the total is the least useful number on the page. What matters is the split: the basic salary on one side, and housing, transport and other allowances on the other. Your gratuity at the end of service is calculated on the basic salary, not the total, and so are most increments and some leave payments. Two offers with the same total can be worth very different amounts over five years if one carries a higher basic. Before you sign, ask for the split in writing, check what happens to allowances during unpaid leave, and confirm whether the flight ticket and medical insurance sit inside or outside the package. The offer letter is a contract - read the small numbers, not just the headline.
