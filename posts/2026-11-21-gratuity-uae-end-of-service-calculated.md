# Gratuity in the UAE: How Your End-of-Service Pay Is Really Calculated

Every expat working in the UAE has heard of gratuity, but few know how it is actually worked out - and the number is often smaller than people expect. Gratuity is calculated on your basic salary, not your total package, and it builds up by year of service: 21 days of basic salary per year for the first five years, then 30 days per year after that. Leave without completing a full year and the calculation is pro-rated by the months you worked. What catches people out is the split: a contract with a high total but a low basic salary produces a much smaller gratuity than a contract where the basic is the bigger share. Before you sign any offer, work out the gratuity on the basic figure, not the headline total - it is part of your pay, spread over your whole stay.
