# UAE Work Visa in 2026: The 5 Documents Every Applicant Needs First

A UAE work visa starts with paperwork, and the same five documents come up for almost every applicant: a passport valid for at least six months, a signed job offer or employment contract, the entry permit your employer applies for, a medical fitness test once you arrive, and the Emirates ID and labour card applications that follow. The employer handles the entry permit and the labour card; you handle the passport, the medical test and showing up on time. The order matters — the permit comes before the flight, the medical test comes after arrival, and the Emirates ID comes last. Missing one document does not cancel the process, but it does delay it, sometimes by weeks.
