# Why the Same Job Pays Differently in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah

The same job title can pay a different salary in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah - and the difference is not random. Dubai usually pays more for roles tied to trade, tourism and international business, because the employers are bigger and the competition for staff is sharper. Abu Dhabi pays strongly for government, energy and defence-linked roles. Sharjah often pays less in cash but the lower cost of living changes what the salary is actually worth. The package matters as much as the number: housing, transport, annual flights and school fees can be worth more than a pay rise on paper. So before comparing two offers, compare the whole package, in the same emirate, for the same experience level.
